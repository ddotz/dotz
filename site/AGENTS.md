# site/ rules

Read before changing a page: `docs/ui-principles.md` (principles), `web/DESIGN.md` (tokens), `docs/ui-spec.md` (app screens the demos mirror).

- Demo cards (`site/stageshow-src/`) and the app share one pattern; reuse what the app does instead of inventing a look.
- Before adding a section or page, look for an existing one with the same job and extend it.
- One lead per card or section: one bold element.
- Spacing comes from tokens (`web/DESIGN.md`), not literals.
- Copy is natural Korean, written the way a person would say it, with no AI-sounding phrases.
- Never mention 카카오톡 on the public site.
- Before done: side-by-side before/after shots at 390px and 1280px, compared item by item with the request.
